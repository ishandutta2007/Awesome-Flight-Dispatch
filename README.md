# Awesome-Flight-Dispatch

## Top Flight Dispatch Tools Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Aviation Flight Planning, Dispatch, Route Optimization & Operational Intelligence*
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Flight Dispatch Tools**. These tools support flight planning, operational flight plan (OFP) generation, route optimization, weather briefing, performance calculations, crew briefing, filing, and dispatch coordination using aeronautical data, weather, and aircraft performance models.

**Examples** include RocketRoute, ForeFlight Dispatch, Jeppesen FliteDeck / JetPlanner, NAVBLUE N-Flight Planning, Flightkeys, Lido Flight, Leon Dispatch, Awery Dispatch, Smart4Aviation, and Skybook (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom aeronautical data integration, trajectory optimization, and open aviation data processing — ideal for airlines, operators, researchers, flight schools, and developers building transparent aviation solutions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[RocketRoute](https://www.rocketroute.com/)**  
  Integrated worldwide flight planning, filing, briefing, charts, performance, and dispatch platform for pilots, business aviation, and airlines.

- **[ForeFlight Dispatch](https://foreflight.com/)**  
  Cloud-based dispatch and flight operations suite with planning, document workflows, briefing, and seamless EFB integration for flight departments.

- **[Jeppesen FliteDeck / JetPlanner](https://ww2.jeppesen.com/)**  
  Industry-standard electronic flight bag, charts, and operational flight planning tools with performance calculations and dispatch deliverables.

- **[NAVBLUE N-Flight Planning](https://www.navblue.aero/)**  
  Airline-grade flight planning and operations platform (Airbus) supporting route optimization, performance, and controlled OFP generation.

- **[Flightkeys](https://www.flightkeys.com/)**  
  Advanced flight planning and trajectory optimization software focused on fuel efficiency and operational cost reduction for airlines.

- **[Lido Flight (Lufthansa Systems)](https://www.lhsystems.com/)**  
  Comprehensive flight planning, dispatch, and operations management suite with route optimization and operational control tools.

- **[Leon Dispatch](https://leon.aero/)**  
  Aviation operations management software for charter and scheduled operators covering dispatch, crew, scheduling, and compliance.

- **[Awery Dispatch](https://awery.com/)**  
  Integrated aviation management and dispatch platform supporting flight operations, maintenance, and commercial workflows.

- **[Smart4Aviation](https://www.smart4aviation.aero/)**  
  Suite of flight operations, dispatch, and crew management solutions for airlines with real-time operational intelligence.

- **[Skybook](https://www.skybook.aero/)**  
  Electronic flight bag and flight operations platform providing briefing, documentation, and dispatch support for crews and operators.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Little Navmap](https://github.com/albar965/littlenavmap)**  
  Free open-source flight planner, moving map, airport search, and navigation tool with procedure support and multi-simulator export formats.

- **[OpenFlights](https://github.com/jpatokal/openflights)**  
  Website and dataset for storing flight information, rendering paths on a world map, calculating statistics, plus free airline, airport, and route data.

- **[navplan.ch](https://github.com/opacopac/navplan)**  
  Open-source VFR flight planning web application integrating aeronautical data, weather, and maps (focused on Switzerland with extensible architecture).

- **[openAIP](https://github.com/openAIP)**  
  Crowd-sourced open aeronautical information platform providing airspace, airports, navaids, and related data with parsers and tools.

- **[OpenAP / openap-top](https://github.com/junzis/openap-top)**  
  Open aircraft performance models and trajectory optimization toolkit for fuel-optimal, wind-optimal, and custom-cost flight trajectories.

- **[AirTrail](https://github.com/johanohly/AirTrail)**  
  Modern open-source personal flight tracking system with interactive maps, statistics, multi-user support, and import from multiple sources.

- **[tangram (open-aviation)](https://github.com/open-aviation/tangram)**  
  Modular open platform for real-time multidisciplinary aviation research, data fusion (Mode-S, weather), and plugin-based tooling.

- **[AeroPlan](https://github.com/Kiptoo-Deus/AeroPlan)**  
  C++/Qt flight planner using A* routing, real-time maps, weather, and export to MSFS, X-Plane, and other formats.

- **[flight-dispatch-center](https://github.com/anldrms/flight-dispatch-center)**  
  Professional aviation flight planning system for simulators with real navigation data, live weather, and multi-format export.

- **[HyPlan](https://github.com/ryanpavlick/hyplan)**  
  Open-source Python library for planning airborne remote sensing campaigns, including flight lines, performance, and logistics.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- **[Open Flightmaps](https://www.openflightmaps.org/)** and related aeronautical mapping data projects.
- **[OurAirports](https://ourairports.com/)** open airport and runway databases widely used in planning tools.
- **[OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org/)** tools and APIs for live and historical ADS-B flight tracking data.
- **[VFR Flight Planner libraries](https://github.com/topics/flightplan)** and community route generators.
- **QSimPlanner-style** open fuel and performance calculators adapted for real or simulated operations.
- Many community **METAR/TAF parsers**, **AIXM/OpenAIR converters**, and **trajectory optimization notebooks**.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Little Navmap** data pipelines, **OpenAP** performance models, **openAIP** / **OurAirports** datasets, **OpenSky** tracking, **InfluxDB + Grafana** dashboards, and **Ollama** or local LLMs for intelligent dispatch assistants and predictive planning platforms.
